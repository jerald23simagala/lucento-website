# Lucento site concepts

Three first-viewport studies, judged before the site was built. Screenshots: `a-desktop.png`, `a-mobile.png`, and the same for `b` and `c`. The routes that produced them are not part of the site.

## A — The Editorial Object

**Thesis.** Lucento is a printed plate: the month is a word too large for the page, and the phone is a numbered figure.

**Atmosphere.** Warm proofing paper, ink, one spot of sunlight yellow. A studio wall, not a magazine grid.

**Composition.** Crop marks, a vertical running title, italic “month,” colliding with a tilted, cropped phone. The mascot is a small captioned plate.

**Type.** Fraunces for the word, IBM Plex Mono for the margin.

**Color.** Paper `#e4dccb`, ink `#1c140e`, spot `#f0b429`.

**Product.** The real Home screen, as Fig. 01.

**Mascot.** A plate in the margin, not a sticker beside a headline.

**Transitions.** Would have been page numbers and thickening rules.

**Why Lucento.** The month beside the spend, the Rising Crest’s sunlight, the keeper’s torch.

**How it differs.** Flat, typographic, and printed. No room, no enlarged UI.

**Novelty.** Pass, with a reservation. The cropped phone and plate are a signature, and the page is interesting before the sentence is read. Warm oversized italic is also a common recent marketing look, so it was not the safest way to escape a generic redesign.

## B — The Living Universe

**Thesis.** The keeper’s torch is the only light, and the month is written on the back wall of that room.

**Atmosphere.** A dark interior. The character is architecture. Not a cartoon world.

**Composition.** “October” cropped by the top edge, the mascot larger than the frame, the phone standing in the remaining dark, the sentence set vertically.

**Type.** Syne for the wall and the spine, Plex Mono for the ledger line.

**Color.** Night `#0b1018`, torch gold, ivory.

**Product.** The Home screen at near phone size, in the scene.

**Mascot.** The subject of the room.

**Transitions.** Would have been moving from the dark room onto a lit ledger page.

**Why Lucento.** The orb, crest, scarf, satchel, and torch are the brand. The light is the record.

**How it differs.** Spatial and character-led. Not a typeset page, not the UI as the page.

**Novelty.** Rejected. The ingredients are specific, but the arrangement still reads as a dark hero: character, device, slogan, button. It does not earn a first viewport that could only be Lucento.

## C — The Anti-Landing-Page

**Thesis.** There is no pitch above the product. The first viewport is the app, enlarged until the frame crops it.

**Atmosphere.** A light table. Clinical, dense, quiet.

**Composition.** Home and Budgets butted together and scaled past the viewport. A vertical spine carries the name and the demo link. A film counter names the screens. No headline column.

**Type.** Plus Jakarta Sans, because it is already the app. Mono only for the spine and counter.

**Color.** The app’s own ivory, navy, and sunlight. No separate marketing palette on the product.

**Product.** The product is the entire picture. Example October records, labeled as such.

**Mascot.** Inside the budget card, where the app already stands them.

**Transitions.** Would be moving from one enlarged screen to the next.

**How it differs.** No editorial word, no character scene. The interface is the composition.

**Why Lucento.** The pace bars, categories, wallets, and the keeper on the budget card are the real app.

**Novelty.** Pass. It is not a centered headline over a floating dashboard. The cropped, oversized UI is recognizable before any sentence. It uses the product as the picture. It is calm enough to use.

## Choice

C, carried through the whole site as one idea: **the record, enlarged, with notes in the margin.**

The opening is the app cropped to the viewport, with the keeper standing in the margin rather than in a hero. Later sections change job — a sentence set as type, two full-width pace bars, a night column for what stays on the phone, an index, endnotes — and they do not share one card style. The mascot appears where the product already gives them a job (pace, checking rows, a missing page), not as a sticker on every block.

B was rejected rather than averaged in. A’s spot color and marginal captions remain only as the annotation voice (mono labels, a sunlight underline), not as a second layout system.
