# Lucento logo concepts (round 1)

Four directions, each tied to the mascot (a golden orb with a hair crest who
carries a crystal-topped torch whose light reflects financial health) and to
managing money. Files are in `brand/logo-concepts/`: a day mark, a night mark and
an app icon per concept. These are concept artwork, not production files; the
chosen direction's wordmark would be redrawn as outlines.

| # | Name | Idea | Wordmark |
|---|------|------|----------|
| 1 | Keeper’s Torch | The L is the torch: navy stem and foot, faceted sunlight crystal on top. The mascot’s light becomes the letter. | Manrope 800, lowercase |
| 2 | Sunrise Coin | A blue coin with the sun rising over a horizon, ledger lines below it. Money, morning, optimism. | Fraunces 600, title case |
| 3 | Rising Crest | Three bars that read as a growth chart and as the mascot’s hair crest; the tallest catches the light. | Sora 600, lowercase |
| 4 | Daylight Dial | A budget gauge like the app’s rings, the mascot’s light at its tip, an L inside. A dial, not an orbital ring. | Manrope 700, uppercase, tracked |
