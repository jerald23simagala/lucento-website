# Lucento logo concepts (round 1)

Four directions, each tied to the mascot (a golden orb with a hair crest who
carries a crystal-topped torch whose light reflects financial health) and to
managing money. Files are in `brand/logo-concepts/`: a day mark, a night mark and
an app icon per concept. These are concept artwork, not production files; the
chosen direction's wordmark would be redrawn as outlines.

| # | Name | Idea | Wordmark |
|---|------|------|----------|
| 1 | Keeper’s Torch | The L is the torch: navy stem and foot, faceted sunlight crystal on top. The mascot’s light becomes the letter. | Manrope 800, lowercase |
| 2 | Sunrise Coin | A blue coin with the sun rising over a horizon, ledger lines below it. Money, morning, optimism. | Fraunces 600, title case |
| 3 | Rising Crest | Three bars that read as a growth chart and as the mascot’s hair crest; the tallest catches the light. | Sora 600, lowercase |
| 4 | Daylight Dial | A budget gauge like the app’s rings, the mascot’s light at its tip, an L inside. A dial, not an orbital ring. | Manrope 700, uppercase, tracked |

Design canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/R4eBjAjKh9yTPKA73MPVxb

# Round 2: revised Sunrise Coin and Rising Crest

Assets: `brand/logo-concepts/round-2/` (see its README). Canvas: "Round 2" page of the
design canvas linked above. No direction has been chosen.

| # | Name | Revised from | Idea |
|---|------|--------------|------|
| 2a | Daybreak Arch | Sunrise Coin | The coin becomes an open arch, a window onto first light, with the sun rising inside. Clarity and awareness, balanced by symmetry. |
| 2b | Sunrise Path | Sunrise Coin | The sun on a level horizon with a winding path leading toward it. Intentional progress you choose. |
| 3a | Crest Sweep | Rising Crest | The bars become three swept strokes leaning forward, like the mascot's crest in motion; the lead stroke catches the light. |
| 3b | Orb Crest | Rising Crest | The mascot's head reduced to an orb and a sunlit quiff. Distinctive and personal, least literal about money. |

Keeper's Torch (1) and Daylight Dial (4) are unchanged in shape and were tested with the
same criteria: silhouette, 16 px legibility, monochrome, symbolism, clichés, mascot link,
Android icon fit and wordmark pairing.

# Direction chosen: Rising Crest

The owner selected Rising Crest, following their own reference sheet
(`brand/logo/reference/rising-crest-reference.png`). The production vector set is in
`brand/logo/` (see its README). The website header and favicon use it. The Android app
has not been changed.
